== 1. Sketching on faces and construction planes

*New Sketch* accepts not only the three origin planes but any *flat face* of a
body. Hover a face: it lights up; click it and the sketch lies on it. The face's
edges are not part of the sketch, but *Project* (kbd:[P]) copies any edge, face
outline, vertex or axis into it as linked geometry (it follows the model when it
changes). Dimension your new curves against the projected ones.

A face can disappear when an earlier feature changes (a fillet eats the edge, a
shell removes the face). A *construction plane* does not. *Model › Construct ›
Offset Plane* creates a plane parallel to an origin plane or a face at a
distance, and the distance can be an expression: the lid in the task sits on an
offset plane at `box_h + lid_gap`.

.An offset plane at a parametric distance, with the lid's sketch and body on it
image::offset-plane-dialog.png[width=800, link=../../../images/solidcraft/offset-plane-dialog.png]

Sketch axes on the origin planes: on *XY*, sketch x and y are world X and Y. On
*XZ* sketch x is world X and sketch y is world **−Z** (so a point at sketch
`[0, -10]` is 10 mm up). On *YZ* sketch x is world Y and sketch y is world Z.
When something extrudes the wrong way, this is usually why; the *Reverse* box in
the dialog or a negative distance fixes it.

== 2. More features

[cols="1,3,2",options="header"]
|===
|Feature |What it does |When to prefer it

|*Revolve* |turns a profile around an axis (a sketch line, an origin axis, an
edge); angle 360° or less |anything round: knobs, bottles, pulleys, wheels. One
sketch of half the cross-section instead of many circles.

|*Shell* |hollows a body: pick the faces to open, set the wall thickness
|boxes, cases, cups. Shell *after* the outer shape is final (including outer
fillets), so the inside follows.

|*Chamfer* |cuts an edge at 45° (or two distances) |the bottom edge of a print
(elephant foot), lids that must slide on, anything that needs no support
material. Fillet on the top, chamfer on the bottom.

|*Mirror* |copies features or bodies across a plane |symmetric parts: model
one half, mirror it. Changes to the half propagate.

|*Rectangular / Circular Pattern* |repeats features, bodies or components with a
count and spacing (or a count on a circle) |vents, holes, teeth, grips. The
count can be an *expression*.

|*Hole* |a drilled, counterbored or countersunk hole on a face, positioned by
two edge distances, optionally with a thread |screw holes. Easier to edit than a
circle in a sketch, and knows about M3/M4 sizes.

|*Extrude Cut* |extrude with operation *Cut*; *Through All* reaches through
every face in the way |slots, windows, text. The *targets* field chooses which
bodies get cut when several overlap.

|*Combine* |join, cut or intersect two bodies |a lid cut from the box body's
shape, a mould from a part.
|===

=== Several bodies in one design

A design may hold any number of bodies; the browser's *Bodies* folder lists them.
Extrude and Revolve offer *New Body* when the new material does not touch an
existing body, *Join* when it does. A *Cut* asks which bodies it should cut
(*Targets*); hidden bodies are never cut. Rename bodies in the browser
(kbd:[F2]) as soon as you have two, and hide one with its eye when you work on
the other.

For a printed part made of two pieces (box and lid) this is enough. Components
and joints, the assembly tools, are a short topic in lesson 3.

== 3. Expressions that design

Lesson 1 used parameters to *rename* numbers. Now the expressions do real work:

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Expression |Why

|`vent_count = floor((box_w - 2 * wall - vent_pitch) / vent_pitch)`
|as many vent slots as fit, leaving a wall at each end; `floor` makes it an
integer, which a pattern count must be

|`lid_inner = box_w + 2 * clearance`
|a clearance *per side* is added twice

|`corner_r = min(thickness, hole_inset / 2)`
|a limit: a fillet larger than the material around it fails

|`wall = max(2 * nozzle, 1.2 mm)`
|a lower bound that depends on the printer

|`angle / 2`, `90 deg - draft`
|angles have their own unit; an angle where a length is expected is an error,
not a silent conversion

|`box_h - vent_margin`, `-(box_h - vent_margin)`
|signs matter on the XZ plane (sketch y is −Z)
|===

Three rules that keep a design editable:

. *One number, one place.* If `wall` appears in a sketch, an extrude and a shell,
  there is exactly one parameter `wall` and three references to it.
. *Derive, don't repeat.* `lid_h`, `vent_margin`, `pitch` are expressions of the
  few numbers that really are decisions.
. *Guard.* Any count or radius that can go to zero or below needs `max()`,
  `min()` or a margin. The sweep tool in lesson 3 finds the rest.

*Manage › Parameters › Param Graph* draws who uses what, which is the quickest
way to find a dimension that still holds a plain number.

== 4. Looking inside

*Inspect › Section Analysis* cuts the view with a plane (XY, XZ, YZ or a
construction plane, with an offset) and hatches the cut faces. Use it to check a
wall thickness, a clearance between lid and box, a hidden hole. The cut is only
in the view; the model is untouched. *Inspect › Measure* (kbd:[I]) with one or
two selected items gives length, area, diameter, distance and angle.

=== Step 1 and 2: body and shell (10 min)

. New design. *Parameters*: `box_w = 60`, `box_l = 40`, `box_h = 30`, `wall = 2`
  (comment: "5 perimeters of 0.4 mm").
. Sketch on XY, *Center Rectangle* from the origin; dimension the sides `box_w`
  and `box_l` (type the names). Finish.
. Extrude `box_h`. Rename the body to `Box` in the browser.
. *Shell*: click the top face, thickness `wall`, OK. Section Analysis on XZ to
  admire the wall.

=== Step 3: the lid on a construction plane (15 min)

. Parameters `lid_h = 8`, `clearance = 0.3` (comment: "gap per side"),
  `lid_gap = 15` (comment: "viewing gap only, not printed").
. *Offset Plane* from XY, distance `box_h + lid_gap`, name it `Lid plane`.
. Sketch on *Lid plane*: a centred rectangle, half-sides
  `box_w / 2 + wall + clearance` and `box_l / 2 + wall + clearance`. Finish.
. Extrude `lid_h`, operation *New Body*. Rename the body `Lid`.
. *Shell* the lid: remove its *bottom* face (the one facing the box), thickness
  `wall`.
. Check with Section Analysis that the lid's inner size is the box's outer size
  plus `2 * clearance`.

=== Step 4: vents that count themselves (20 min)

. Parameters `vent_w = 2`, `vent_pitch = vent_w * 3`, `vent_margin = wall * 3`,
  and `vent_count = floor((box_w - 2 * wall - vent_pitch) / vent_pitch)` with
  unit *none*.
. Sketch on *XZ*. A centred rectangle: centre at sketch `[0, -box_h / 2]`,
  corner `[vent_w / 2, -(box_h - vent_margin)]`. Remember sketch y = −Z.
  Finish.
. Extrude: direction *Symmetric*, extent *Through All*, operation *Cut*,
  targets: only *Box* (hide the lid first, or untick it). Rename the feature
  `Vent`.
. *Rectangular Pattern*: object type *Features*, pick `Vent`, direction X,
  quantity `vent_count`, spacing `vent_pitch`, alignment *centre*.
. Change `box_w` to 90 and to 35. The number of vents must change, and no vent
  may cut the corner.

=== Step 5: finishing (10 min)

. *Chamfer* the four top edges of the lid, distance `wall / 2`.
. *Fillet* the four vertical edges of the box, radius `wall * 2`. Note the order
  problem: the shell was computed before the fillet, so the inside corners stay
  sharp. Drag the fillet before the shell in the timeline (or edit the design so
  the fillet comes first) and compare the sections.
. Hide `Lid plane` and the sketches. Save. Export *3MF* (both bodies as separate
  parts) and an STL of each body.

=== Hand in

`box.sc` with your own sizes, plus a screenshot of the section view showing the
clearance. Bonus: a `clearance` that depends on a `printer` parameter (`0.2`
for the Prusa, `0.3` for the Voron).

== Common problems

* *The lid sketch shows no profile.* The rectangle is not closed or lies on the
  wrong plane. Check the browser: the sketch must be under *Lid plane*.
* *The vent cut removed the lid too.* The cut's targets included both bodies.
  Edit the feature and set targets to *Box*.
* *Pattern count must be a number.* `vent_count` has a unit; set it to *none*,
  or `floor()` is missing so the value is 8.33.
* *Shell fails after fillet.* The fillet radius is smaller than the wall, so the
  inner offset has no room. Use `wall * 2` or more.
* *Section Analysis shows nothing.* The plane does not pass through the body; set
  an offset, or pick XZ instead of YZ.
